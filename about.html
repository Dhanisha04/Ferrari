ABOUT FERRARI

The story of the Prancing Horse

OUR HISTORY

Founded by Enzo Ferrari in 1939 out of the Alfa Romeo race division as Auto Avio Costruzioni, the company built its first car in 1940. However, the company's inception as an automotive manufacturer is usually recognized in 1947, when the first Ferrari-badged car was completed.

In 1960, Ferrari was reorganized as a public company under the name SEFAC S.p.A. Early in 1969, Fiat took a 50% stake in Ferrari. An immediate result was an increase in available investment funds, and work started at once on a factory extension intended to transfer production from Fiorano.

FERRARI MUSEUMS

Discover the history and passion behind the Prancing Horse at our museums in Maranello and Modena. The museums offer an exciting and complete journey through Ferrari's legendary story, from its very first steps to the latest technological innovations.

Visitors can admire some of the most iconic cars in automotive history, including Formula 1 single-seaters, GT cars, and sports prototypes that have written unforgettable pages in motorsport history.
